Membership Benefits

Joining the Red Deer Reavers means becoming part of a dynamic community passionate about historical armored combat. Members receive exclusive benefits, including:

  • Discounted gear and training sessions
  • Early access to events and tournaments
  • Opportunities to shape the future of Buhurt in Alberta
