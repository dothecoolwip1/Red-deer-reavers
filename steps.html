Steps to Success

Our Roadmap

Our roadmap ensures sustainable growth and development for the Buhurt community in Alberta. Each phase is designed to achieve key milestones that bring us closer to our vision.

Phase Timeline

home
Jan 2023 - Dec 2023: Foundation

Incorporation, initial fundraising, and participation in the World Tournament.

In the first year, we focus on establishing the foundation of Red Deer Reavers. This includes legal incorporation, securing our first major sponsors, and ensuring our team is ready for the World Tournament.

expand
Jan 2024 - Dec 2024: Growth

Hosting regional tournaments and expanding youth programs.

In Year 2, we look to expand regionally with tournaments and further grow our youth programs. We aim to get more individuals involved in the sport while ensuring sustainability for future growth.

group
Jan 2025 - Dec 2027: Leadership

Building a training facility and establishing provincial leadership.

Building a permanent facility dedicated to training and developing new talent. Establishing leadership across Alberta for greater organizational strength.

location_city
Jan 2028 - Dec 2029: National Impact

Gaining national recognition and hosting Canadian championships.

By this time, the Red Deer Reavers will be a national force in the Buhurt community, and we'll be hosting the Canadian championships.

star
Jan 2030 - Dec 2032: Legacy

Creating a permanent Buhurt arena and hosting international tournaments.

Our final goal is to build a state-of-the-art Buhurt arena, ensuring the legacy of the Red Deer Reavers is felt for generations to come.
