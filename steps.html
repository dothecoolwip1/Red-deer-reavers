Steps to Success

Our Roadmap

Our roadmap ensures sustainable growth and development for the Buhurt community in Alberta. Each phase is designed to achieve key milestones that bring us closer to our vision.

Phase Timeline

Jan 2023 - Dec 2023: Foundation

Incorporation, initial fundraising, and participation in the World Tournament.

Establishing the legal foundation for the Red Deer Reavers, creating partnerships, and ensuring readiness for major events.

Jan 2024 - Dec 2024: Growth

Hosting regional tournaments and expanding youth programs.

Focus on engaging the community through events, fostering youth development, and strengthening regional involvement in Buhurt.

Jan 2025 - Dec 2025: Leadership

Building a training facility and establishing provincial leadership.

Creating a centralized training hub and mentoring new leaders to solidify the organization's impact in Alberta.

Jan 2026 - Dec 2027: National Recognition

Gaining national recognition and hosting Canadian championships.

Promoting Buhurt at a national level by hosting premier events and establishing a strong competitive presence in Canada.

Jan 2028 - Dec 2030: Legacy

Creating a permanent Buhurt arena and hosting international tournaments.

Leaving a lasting legacy with a dedicated arena, positioning Alberta as a global hub for Historical Armored Combat.
