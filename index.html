Red Deer Reavers Non-Profit Plan

Step 1: Define the Mission and Vision

  • Draft the mission statement: "To support the Red Deer Reavers and promote Buhurt through training resources, competitions, and youth engagement."
  • Set initial goals:
    • Raise funds for practice space, gear, and travel.
    • Establish a sustainable structure for the team.
    • Prepare for the World Tournament in Houston, Texas.

Step 2: Governance Setup

  • Board of Directors:
    • Minimum 3 roles required:
      • President: Oversees the organization and ensures goals are met.
      • Treasurer: Manages finances, including donations and expenses.
      • Secretary: Handles documentation, meeting minutes, and correspondence.
    • Optional roles:
      • Fundraising Coordinator: Develops and oversees fundraising efforts.
      • Youth Program Coordinator: Manages future youth involvement plans.
  • Recruit members for the board:
    • Discuss with teammates and trusted individuals who are interested in leadership roles.

Step 3: Legal and Administrative

  • Register the Non-Profit:
    • File incorporation documents for a non-profit organization in Alberta.
    • Choose the name: Red Deer Reavers.
    • Draft and submit bylaws outlining how the organization operates (e.g., elections, decision-making, meetings).
  • Get a Business Number:
    • Apply for a business number with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).
  • Charitable Status (Optional):
    • Register with CRA to issue tax-deductible receipts for donations (can be done later).

Step 4: Fundraising and Revenue

  • Develop funding strategies:
    • Approach companies where team members are employed for donations or sponsorships.
    • Organize fundraising events (e.g., Buhurt demonstrations or tournaments).
    • Launch crowdfunding campaigns on platforms like GoFundMe.
  • Build a donor-friendly structure:
    • Offer sponsorship packages (e.g., logo placement, event mentions).
    • Recognize supporters on social media and team materials.

Step 5: Operational Planning

  • Gear and Space:
    • Prioritize purchasing training and competition gear.
    • Secure a rental practice space after initial funding.
  • Youth Program Development:
    • Plan outreach efforts to schools and youth organizations.
    • Develop a beginner-friendly program using soft kit training gear.
  • Travel Planning:
    • Estimate costs for attending the World Tournament in Houston, Texas (flights, accommodation, gear transport).
    • Start a specific fundraiser for this goal.

Step 6: Community Engagement

  • Social Media Presence:
    • Use platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube to share updates, events, and achievements.
    • Create a website with donation links and team information.
  • Local Events:
    • Host exhibitions or open houses to raise awareness about Buhurt.
    • Partner with local community centers or sports clubs to attract interest.

Step 7: Timeline

  • First 3 Months:
    • Finalize incorporation and board setup.
    • Begin initial fundraising and secure the business number.
    • Build a social media presence.
  • Next 3 Months:
    • Launch fundraising events and campaigns.
    • Secure practice space and gear.
    • Begin travel preparations for Houston.
  • 6 Months to 1 Year:
    • Attend the World Tournament.
    • Initiate the youth program pilot.

Step 8: Sustainability

  • Regularly evaluate goals and finances.
  • Explore additional funding sources, including grants.
  • Build partnerships with local businesses and organizations.
