Red Deer Reavers

Bringing History to Life Through Sport

Welcome to the Reavers

The Red Deer Reavers are committed to growing Historical Armored Combat in Alberta. Join us as we train, compete, and connect communities through this dynamic medieval sport.

Our Mission

To empower the Red Deer Reavers and the broader Buhurt community through training resources, competitions, and youth engagement, preserving the sport's historical roots while advancing its modern athletic potential.

Our Vision

To establish Red Deer, Alberta, as a thriving hub for Historical Armored Combat, fostering a community of athletes, enthusiasts, and supporters dedicated to excellence, camaraderie, and the preservation of medieval history.

What We Offer

group
Youth Programs

Inspiring the next generation through engaging and safe historical combat education.

We offer hands-on training, safety practices, and team-building activities for young enthusiasts looking to experience the thrill of Buhurt.

event
Community Events

Engaging and educating through interactive and exciting events.

Our events bring together enthusiasts from all backgrounds to celebrate medieval history, learn combat techniques, and bond as a community.

sports
Competitive Excellence

Building champions who embody skill, dedication, and sportsmanship.

Our team competes at the highest level, aiming to represent Red Deer in national and international Buhurt competitions, showcasing both athleticism and honor.
