What is an AGM?

The Annual General Meeting is the main yearly business meeting for the Red Deer Reavers Guild. It gives members a chance to review how the society is doing, elect leadership, review finances, raise issues and help decide where the team goes next.

Discuss membership, operations, events, gear, fundraising and team priorities.

Future Direction

Bring forward ideas, concerns and goals for the next year and beyond.

What to expect: The meeting will follow an agenda, attendance and minutes will be recorded, members will be able to discuss agenda items, and formal votes will be documented. The current bylaws give each member one vote and allow proxy voting with prior written notice. Members who cannot attend in person may also participate through an approved documented voting method.
